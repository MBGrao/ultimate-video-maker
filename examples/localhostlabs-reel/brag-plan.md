# Brag Plan: Localhost Labs, "Likh ke do" reel

## What is this app?
Localhost Labs (localhostlabs.tech) is a custom software and automation studio in Multan, Pakistan. It builds internal tools, CRM/ERP systems, integrations and apps for businesses, and works white-label for agencies.

## The angle
A Format E (Do / Don't) explainer from the reel playbook README. Pakistani small-business owners are scared of the advance-paid developer who disappears. Localhost Labs' real process answers that fear point by point: a free first call, a paid discovery that puts scope, milestones and a fixed cost in writing, milestone-based builds, no hourly billing, and regular demos of working software. The reel teaches the viewer three things to demand from any developer, and every one of them is something the site already promises.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
Spoken: "Poora advance diya, aur developer ghayab?"
On screen: the words build up big and kinetic, while a chat shows three unanswered "Bhai update?" messages and the developer's avatar fades to a dashed outline on "ghayab".
Hook score: 77.8 STRONG on the English gloss ("You paid the full advance. The developer vanished."), from `ig-reel/hookscore.py`.

## Key moments (the middle)
1. Story: Sana (fictional boutique owner) hands over her app on one phone call. A document shows "Scope ____ / Deadline ____", and both get stamped ✗.
2. Three months later: a giant counter rolls DIN 1 → DIN 90, then "Bas 2 din aur bhai 🙏" arrives.
3. Three demands, each ✗ → ✓:
   - Likh kar: a project plan with Scope ✓ Milestones ✓ Fixed price ✓ (site: "scope, milestones and a fixed cost in writing")
   - Milestone payment: a progress track that pays per milestone (site: "A milestone-based build", "No hourly billing")
   - Weekly demo: a working app on a phone, weeks ticking (site: "Regular demos of real, clickable progress, not slide decks"; OG image: "weekly demos")

## Outro / punchline
"Yaad rakho: andaaza nahi, likha hua plan." Then SAVE → the real 3-step process from the site (Free 30-minute call → Paid discovery → Development) → logo, @localhostlabs, localhostlabs.tech.

## User flow worth showing
The site's own "How every project starts" stepper (Step 01 Free · Step 02 Paid, fixed fee · Step 03 Contract), recreated in the brand's UI style.

- Music cue guidance: preset read (`happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`). Every scene cut is snapped to the beat grid, and the voice enters 0.14 s after each cut.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none

- Audio-coupled moments: chat bubbles "pop", ✗ stamps thud, DIN counter ticks, ✓ checks, milestone coins, taps on save/follow
- Restraint rule: no SFX on top of a spoken keyword; small repeated sounds stay under −18 dB
